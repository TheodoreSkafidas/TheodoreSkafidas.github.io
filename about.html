About Me

I am a newly graduated Electrical Engineer actively seeking full-time opportunities. My expertise lies in bridging the gap between physical hardware and embedded software, translating concepts from multi-layer board layouts into fully functional, programmed systems.

Education and Background

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, where I focused on embedded systems, PCB design, and firmware development. My academic projects often involved hands-on work with microcontrollers, sensors, and actuators, allowing me to gain practical experience in both hardware and software integration.

My Engineering Philosophy

I have always had a strong preference for hands-on learning and tactile applications. While simulation and modeling are crucial first steps, I thrive when engaged in active electronic circuit design and hardware debugging. Rather than being confined behind a screen, I prefer roles where my contributions to the team involve bringing physical printed circuit boards to life on the bench.

Beyond the Lab

When I am not working on hardware or firmware projects, I enjoy applying my attention to detail to building and painting plastic scale models. To completely unplug and recharge, you can usually find me outdoors enjoying some freshwater fishing at a local clearing.
